# GNN as Dynamical Systems

Master's thesis research at St. Petersburg State University, Faculty of Mathematics and Mechanics, Department of Applied Cybernetics.

This repository contains code, experiments, and thesis materials for the study of Graph Neural Networks interpreted through dynamical systems theory. The research focuses on stability analysis, convergence properties, numerical integration methods, and reproducibility of experiments.
